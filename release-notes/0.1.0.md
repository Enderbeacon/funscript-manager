First packaged build.

- Libraries of video and audio with their companion files: funscripts (multi-axis, several versions per media) and subtitles. Each media keeps a `.meta.json` beside it and stays readable without the app.
- Tags, performers, studios, script authors and playlists, with an advanced search over all of them.
- EroScripts posts: paste a link, see what it carries, download what you pick. Downloaders for pixeldrain, mega, gofile, Google Drive, Dropbox, MediaFire, and the usual video sites.
- Playback in the built-in player, mpv, MPC-HC or HereSphere, with the script driven by the built-in player or MultiFunPlayer.
