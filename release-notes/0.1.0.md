First release.

- **Libraries** of videos and audio with their scripts and subtitles. Several script versions and axes per video are grouped automatically, and each video keeps a `.meta.json` beside it that stays readable without the app.
- **Organising** with tags, script authors, studios, ratings and playlists, a filter sidebar and an advanced search.
- **EroScripts posts**: paste a link, see its scripts and video sources, and download what you pick. The post's tags, author and link are filed with the video.
- **Downloads** from pixeldrain, mega, gofile, Google Drive, Dropbox, MediaFire, video sites and direct links, in a queue that picks up again after a restart. yt-dlp and ffmpeg are downloaded on first run.
- **Playback** in the built-in player, mpv, MPC-HC or HereSphere. Scripts drive TCode devices or The Handy from the built-in script player, or go through MultiFunPlayer.
- **Updates** are checked from the About page, on the stable or beta channel.
- In English, Chinese, Japanese, German and French.
