## VR panel

- **Seek from inside VR.** The now-playing strip carries the heatmap scrubber, sized for a laser pointer, so you can see where you are in a video and jump elsewhere without taking the headset off. It folds down to a thin playhead when you want the room back.
- **Either hand.** Reach for a panel with whichever hand is nearer and that hand uses it. Pointing the other hand at it first is no longer needed, and a resting hand no longer fights the one that is moving.
