- **More downloads work**: gofile works again, downloads follow the system proxy, and more video sites can be downloaded at the quality you choose. Folder links from cloud hosts are expanded into their files.
- **Site checks**: when a site asks for a captcha or a browser check, open it in a window and pass it; the waiting downloads then retry on their own.
- **Download queue**: running and waiting jobs share one Downloading tab, and the list beside posts can pause, resume and cancel jobs.
- **Posts**: new posts appear at the top, "Add a link" is easier to find, and a link check that can't decide says why and offers to open the site. Notes beside links no longer show leftover markup. Scripts in posts with several videos can be paired by hand.
- **Built-in player**: plays formats the browser can't by converting them on the fly, keeps its position when moved to its own window and back, and keeps the playing video when the queue is cleared.
- **Library**: repair multi-axis scripts that were split into separate versions, remove items from the library from the context menu, and see what a merge will produce before merging.
- **Safe-for-work switch** in the top bar blurs covers and previews until you point at one.
- Errors now show beside what caused them, or as a notice at the top of the window.
