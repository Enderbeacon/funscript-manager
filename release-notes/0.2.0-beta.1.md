- **VR panel (beta)**: browse your library and start videos from inside SteamVR, without taking the headset off.
  - The panel shows up whenever SteamVR is running. Point a controller at it to use it; the rest of the time your controllers work in the player as usual.
  - Browse with large covers, search, pinned tags and favourites, choose a script version, and play. Videos play in HereSphere, which needs to be in your player list.
  - A queue with shuffle and repeat, and playlists you can play, reorder, and save the queue into.
  - A second panel beside it holds the script player.
  - Hold grip on a panel to move it. The main panel can hang on your left wrist; turn the back of your right hand towards you to bring it back.
  - Size, height and opacity can be set for each panel.
- Theme and language changes now reach detached player windows right away.
- Includes everything in 0.1.2.
