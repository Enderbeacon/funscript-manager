## Now with full VR support

**VR video on your monitor.** 180°, 360° and fisheye files — MKX200, MKX220, VRCA220, RF52 — play as a picture you can look around, no headset needed. Thumbnails and previews stop showing warped double images.

**Your library inside SteamVR.** Browse, search, queue and play without taking the headset off, with the script player on a panel of its own. Out of beta, on for everyone.

## Fixes

- Unplugging a device mid-script no longer throws a Windows error box. Plug it back in and it picks the script up where it left off.
- Theme and language changes reach detached player windows right away.
