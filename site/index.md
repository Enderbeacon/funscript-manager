<FeatureRow shot="detail" alt="A media entry with several script versions and axes" title="Every script, every version, every axis">

Point it at a folder. Scripts and subtitles next to a video are grouped with it on their own: multi-axis sets, alternative versions from different authors, subtitles in several languages.

- Pick the default version, or switch while it plays
- A single-axis script can borrow the other axes from a multi-axis one
- Everything is written to a small file beside the video, so nothing is lost if the app goes

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow shot="filters" alt="Searching tags, authors and studios at once" title="Find it again in seconds" flip>

Tags with parents and aliases, video and script authors, studios, ratings, duration and folders. Narrow the list from the sidebar, or build nested conditions and save them.

- Search every kind of name at once with Ctrl K, then include or exclude
- Batch edit tags, ratings and favourites across a selection
- Playlists you can order, shuffle and export

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow shot="posts" alt="A parsed forum post with its video and script sources" title="From forum post to library">

Paste an EroScripts link. The post's scripts and video sources are listed with a quick check of which links still work. Pick what you want and the tags, authors and source are filed with the download.

- pixeldrain, MEGA, Gofile, Google Drive, Dropbox, MediaFire and more
- A resumable download queue that survives restarts
- Already have the video? Match your entries to the posts they came from

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow shot="vr-panel" alt="The VR panel and the script player floating in front of a VR video" title="VR on your monitor, and your library in the headset" flip>

VR videos play on your monitor as a view you can look around, with no headset needed. With one on, the library comes with you on a panel inside SteamVR.

- 180°, 360° and fisheye files, MKX200, MKX220, VRCA220 and RF52
- Thumbnails and previews show a normal picture, not two warped halves
- Browse, search, queue and play from the headset, with the script player on its own panel

</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow shot="devices" alt="The script player with a device connected and per-axis settings" title="Press play, and everything follows">

The video plays and your device moves with it, from the script version you picked. No second program to start, no script to load by hand.

- TCode devices over serial, TCP, UDP or WebSocket, and The Handy
- Per-axis ranges, speed limits, motion providers and smart limits
- Already set up with MultiFunPlayer? The app can hand scripts to it instead

</FeatureRow>
