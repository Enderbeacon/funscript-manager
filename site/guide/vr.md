# VR

VR videos play on your monitor as a view you can look around, no headset needed. With a headset on, your library is on a panel inside SteamVR.

## VR video on your monitor

<figure class="screenshot">
  <video src="/media/vr-look.mp4" poster="/media/vr-look.webp" autoplay loop muted playsinline aria-label="Dragging to look around a 180° video in the built-in player"></video>
</figure>

A VR file stores a sphere squashed into a rectangle, usually twice, once for each eye. The built-in player undoes that and shows one eye as a normal view.

- **Drag** the picture to look around
- **Ctrl + wheel** zooms in and out; the wheel alone is still the volume
- **Right-click → VR video → Recentre view** looks straight ahead again

Supported formats: 180°, 360°, fisheye 190°, 200° and 220° (MKX200, MKX220, VRCA220) and RF52, with the eyes side by side, over under, or a single picture.

### Marking a file as VR

A video plays as VR only once it is marked. When the name or the picture's shape looks like VR, the detail panel offers **Mark as VR**. You can also:

- turn on **VR video** in the detail panel, or
- right-click the playing picture and choose **VR video → Projection** and **Eye layout**

<Screenshot name="vr-flat" alt="The picture's menu with VR video, Projection and the formats to choose from" />

The mark is saved with the video. Thumbnails and previews of marked files show one eye as a flat picture.

## The VR panel in SteamVR

<figure class="screenshot">
  <video src="/media/vr-panel.mp4" poster="/media/vr-panel.webp" autoplay loop muted playsinline aria-label="Pointing at the VR panel: filtering by a tag, opening a video, Play next, seeking on the heatmap and opening the queue"></video>
</figure>

With SteamVR running, the panel appears in your headset on its own. It works with any PC VR headset SteamVR supports.

**Before you start:** add **HereSphere** in the player list (see [Video players](/guide/playback)) and start it. Videos started from the panel always play in HereSphere.

### Using it

- Point a controller at the panel to use it. When you point away, the controller goes back to HereSphere.
- **Videos**: large covers, search, favourites and your pinned tags as one-tap filters. Pin more under **All tags**.
- Select a video to see its script versions, then **Play**, **Play next** or **Add to end**.
- **Queue**: move videos up and down, shuffle, repeat, and play or save playlists.
- The seek bar under **Playing** shows the heatmap. Point at it to jump.
- A second panel beside it holds the script player.

### Moving and putting it away

- Hold **grip** while pointing at a panel to move it.
- **On wrist** hangs the main panel on your left wrist. **In front** brings it back.
- **Put away** hides the panel. To bring it back, turn the back of your right hand towards you and point at the button that appears on your wrist.
- The panel puts itself away when a video starts. Turn off **Put away when playing** to keep it up.

**Panel settings** sets size, height and opacity for each panel.
