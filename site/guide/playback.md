# Video players and the queue

## Choosing a player

**Play** sends the video to the player selected in the top bar. Click that button (it reads **Built-in** at first) to open **Video player**, where you can **Add a player** and pick which one is used.

| Player | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Built-in** | Plays inside the app window, VR included ([VR](/guide/vr)). Formats it cannot decode need another player. |
| **mpv** | Install mpv yourself. The app finds it on your PATH, or set **Program** to `mpv.exe`. It is started for you when you press Play. |
| **MPC-HC** | Turn on **Options → Player → Web Interface** in MPC-HC and check the port matches. |
| **HereSphere** | Start HereSphere first; the app cannot start it for you. |

The built-in player can be shrunk, filled to the window, opened in its own window or shown full screen. Subtitles next to the video are offered automatically, and their delay, size, colour and position can be adjusted.

## The bottom bar

The bar at the bottom shows what is playing, with the script's heatmap as the progress bar. From here you can skip, shuffle, repeat and change the volume.

## The queue

The button at the bottom right chooses what plays next:

- **Just this one** stops after the current video
- **Current view** plays through the list you are looking at
- **Your queue** plays videos you added with **Add to queue** or **Play next**

Drag videos onto the queue to add them, and save the queue as a playlist when you want to keep it.
