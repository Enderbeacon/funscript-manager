# Forum posts and downloads

## Download a post

<Screenshot name="posts" alt="A parsed forum post with its video and script sources" />

Open **Download Post**, paste an EroScripts post link and click **Parse**. The post is read and its links are sorted into **Video** and **Scripts**, including mirrors people posted in the replies.

- Links are checked in the background and marked **Link works** or **This link is dead**.
- Tick what you want, choose the library under **Download to**, and click **Download**.
- Links the app cannot fetch, such as a store page, are listed with **Go to site**.

When the downloads finish, the post's title, tags, author and link are filed with the video, and the scripts are attached as versions.

If the video is not available yet, **Add to library, file to follow** saves the scripts and metadata now. The entry waits for its video.

You can also paste direct links to files, one per line.

## Supported sources

- pixeldrain, MEGA, Gofile, Google Drive, Dropbox and MediaFire
- files attached to the forum post
- several video sites, through yt-dlp
- plain direct links

Some video sites need an account for members-only videos. Sign in under **Settings → General → Site sign-in**. Sign-in happens on the site's own page, and only its session cookie is stored.

Most forum posts can be read without an account. For restricted posts, use **Sign in** at the top of the window.

## The download queue

**View all downloads** shows every job with its progress and speed. Downloads can be paused, resumed, retried and cancelled. They resume where they left off after the app is closed and opened again.

**Settings → General → Downloads** sets the preferred video quality. **Network** sets a proxy and a speed limit.

## Matching existing videos to posts

Videos you already had don't know which post they came from. **Post match** searches the forum for each entry and lists the likely posts for you to confirm. A confirmed post fills in tags, authors and links without replacing anything you set.

For a single video, open it and use **Find the post** on the **Source** tab.
